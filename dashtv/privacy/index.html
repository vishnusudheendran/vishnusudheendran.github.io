DashTV Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

DashTV is made by EverInfinite LLC. In short: we don't collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. DashTV has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, such as the screen fit mode and the sites where you turned ad blocking off, are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Websites you visit

DashTV includes a web browser. Sites you open receive the same information they would in any browser, and their own privacy policies apply.

Ad-block updates

DashTV downloads updated filter lists from dashtvfilters.everinfinite.co. These downloads contain no personal information and we don't keep logs of them. The files are delivered through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address only to deliver them. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Screen mirroring and CarPlay

Mirrored video goes from your iPhone straight to your car's screen. It never leaves your devices and is never recorded or uploaded.

Purchases

DashTV Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment and we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

Children

DashTV is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
