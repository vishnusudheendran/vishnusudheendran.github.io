How do I play a video on my car?

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay, open DashTV, play a video in the Browser tab and tap the car button. Confirm the safety notice on the car screen once per drive.

How do I mirror my whole screen?

Open the Mirror tab, tap Mirror Screen, then tap Start Broadcast. Keep DashTV open on the car screen.

How do I send a video from another app?

In YouTube or any app, tap Share, then DashTV. The video opens in DashTV and plays on the car screen.

Why doesn't the YouTube app's Cast button find my car?

Cast needs both devices on the same Wi-Fi network, so it can't reach CarPlay. Share the video to DashTV instead, or use Mirror Screen.

Why is there no picture in the car?

Your car decides when video can show. Until then, you hear the sound.

Can I control playback from the car?

Yes. Play, pause and skip from the steering wheel, the CarPlay screen or the lock screen.

Why won't Netflix play?

Netflix and other protected services only play in their own apps.

There are black bars on my car screen.

In the Mirror tab, pick Fill or Stretch under Car screen.

A website looks broken.

With DashTV Pro, tap the shield in the address bar to turn ad blocking off for that site. The page reloads without blocking.

Does it work with wireless CarPlay?

Yes. DashTV works with wired and wireless CarPlay.
