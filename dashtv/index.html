DashTV

Coming soon to the App Store

DashTV brings your phone's video to the car's screen. Built for passengers and for when you're parked.

What it does

  • Send to Car. Play a video in DashTV's browser and show it on your CarPlay screen.
  • Mirror Screen. Show your whole iPhone screen on the car display.
  • Fit, Fill or Stretch. Use the full screen on any car, from wide displays to compact ones.
  • Ad blocking with DashTV Pro. Updated automatically, with a per-site off switch.
  • Big, simple controls. Play, pause and skip 10 seconds from the phone or the car.

Safety

Video is for passengers and parked vehicles. DashTV asks you to confirm before playing on the car screen. Never watch while driving.
